Stores your items in gravestones when you die instead of dropping them on the ground.

When playing on Medium (Mediumcore) or Hardcore difficulty, dying in Terraria means your items scatter across the ground. This mod automatically stores all your items in a gravestone at your death location.

Features:
- Automatic item storage on death
- Stores inventory, armor, accessories, coins, ammo, and equipment
- Only works on Mediumcore and Hardcore difficulties
- Random gravestone styles
- Works in multiplayer
